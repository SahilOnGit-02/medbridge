# Problem statement

The project addresses fragmented patient histories across hospital systems and the time required to locate the correct person and relevant information. Hospital-specific identifiers are mapped to a canonical MedBridge patient so normalized encounters and clinical entries can be read together.

The UX direction prioritizes visible identity checks, immediate patient search, recent records, compact history, explicit report access and understandable sharing choices. These are design intentions. No reduction in clinical decision time, diagnostic benefit or patient outcome has been measured in this work.

Success for the capstone means demonstrating the implemented journeys with synthetic records, verifying authorization boundaries and reporting limitations honestly. It does not establish clinical safety, regulatory compliance or production reliability.
